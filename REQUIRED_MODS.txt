1. Upload LIB_BaPlayerLocation first (ModId must match exactly).
2. Upload VoogleRoute 0.11.2+ and state LIB BA Player Location 0.11.1+ is required in the Workshop description.
3. Players must subscribe to AND enable both mods.

VoogleRoute does not probe player position itself. Navigation is blocked until LIB_BaPlayerLocation is active.
