[b]Voogle Route 1.0.12[/b]

Voogle Route extends Voogle Maps with on-ground route lines, walking navigation, road-aware driving routes, subway fallback, bookmarks, visit history and a choice of visible driving or fast travel.

This update targets [b]Big Ambitions 1.0, Build 3682[/b]. Visible auto-drive is a beta feature. EA 0.11 has not been revalidated for this release.

[b]What's new in 1.0.12[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Choose each trip[/b] — AUTO-DRIVE now offers WATCH THE DRIVE and FAST TRAVEL before you confirm. Set the default selection in mod options.
[*][b]Visible auto-drive (beta)[/b] — your car follows a fitted street route with native engine, physics, fuel use and gear changes. Movement keys, ESC or STOP DRIVING return control.
[*][b]Driving preferences[/b] — automatic cruising or a manual 20–80 km/h cap; stop for pedestrians; allow speeding up to 80 km/h; run red lights. Settings are saved per save.
[*][b]Road-speed cruising[/b] — normal city cruising targets 50 km/h, with lower speeds for bends, traffic and the vehicle's capabilities. Speeding and red-light overrides default off; stopping for pedestrians defaults on.
[*][b]Revised cornering and cruise control[/b] — adjusted curve speeds, braking anticipation and steering look-ahead, with smoother accelerator control around the target speed.
[*][b]Live dialog availability[/b] — if you open the dialog while moving, it refreshes after you stop or reposition. A failed visible route leaves fast travel selectable.
[*][b]Less repeated work[/b] — valid confirmation plans and traffic/collider lookups are reused; unchanged stationary refusals do not trigger repeated route searches.
[/list]

[b]Using AUTO-DRIVE[/b]

[list]
[*]Set a destination on Voogle Maps and enter your vehicle.
[*]Open AUTO-DRIVE, select WATCH THE DRIVE or FAST TRAVEL, review the details and confirm.
[*]For visible driving, stop on a traffic lane facing its direction. Arrivals are on the road; park manually.
[*]Fast travel keeps the time skip and shows estimated travel time, arrival, distance and fuel before departure. Base taxi multiplier adjusts skip duration.
[/list]

Visible driving does not include reversing, U-turns, automatic parking or elevated/underground driving routes. Choose fast travel if the car or route is unsuitable. Vehicle/obstacle detection remains active when overriding red lights or speed limits; the game's collisions and fines remain active too.

[b]Required Workshop items[/b]

[list]
[*][b]LIB BA Player Location 1.0.0 or newer[/b]
[*][b]LIB BA Unified UI 1.0.0 or newer[/b]
[/list]

Subscribe to and enable both libraries before enabling Voogle Route. They are separate mods and are not bundled into Voogle Route.

[b]Included navigation features[/b]

[list]
[*]Thin on-ground route lines for walking, vehicles, interiors, subway journeys and the city map
[*]Optional auto-walk with walk → subway → walk fallback
[*]City-map DRIVE / WALK shortcuts and searchable bookmarks with category filters
[*]Visit History with route distances, SET, CENTER and bookmark actions
[*]Quick navigation to the last car, home, shop and owned parked vehicles
[*]Big Ambitions 1.0 road data, Hamptons mansion approaches and entrance-first exit guidance
[*]Optional auto-enter on arrival and continued guidance while parking and walking
[*]Draggable panels, configurable shortcuts and separate walking/indoor/vehicle route colors
[*]50–150% UI scale under Options → Mods, applied immediately and saved per save
[*]22 interface languages
[/list]

[b]Optional phone GPS[/b]

Subscribe to and enable [b]Aluna's Phone Pages[/b] to open Voogle Route on the HUD phone. The app offers a heading-up vanilla map, bookmark search, recenter, a full-map shortcut and long-press destination pins. It restores the view after BizMan or the city map; dragging the phone map does not make the character walk.

[b]Installation / update[/b]

Subscribe to Voogle Route and both required libraries, enable them in the game's Mods menu, and fully restart Big Ambitions after Workshop updates finish.
