[b]Voogle Route[/b]

Voogle Route extends Voogle Maps with a clear on-ground route line, walking navigation, road-aware driving routes, subway fallback, bookmarks, visit history and optional fast travel.

Subscribe to and enable [b]LIB BA Player Location 0.11.2 or newer[/b] before enabling Voogle Route.

[b]What's new in 0.11.11[/b]

[list]
[*][b]Flatbed & hand truck fix[/b] — pushed delivery equipment is always treated as walking
[*][b]Auto-drive safety[/b] — cargo tools are blocked before planning, confirmation and teleport
[*][b]Cargo protection[/b] — prevents lost products and carts being moved to random road positions
[*][b]Correct walking origin[/b] — routes follow the player instead of the pushed equipment
[*][b]Last-car shortcut[/b] — hand trucks and flatbeds no longer replace the saved motor vehicle
[/list]

[b]Main features[/b]

[list]
[*]On-ground route line on foot and in motor vehicles
[*]Auto-walk with optional subway fallback
[*]Road-aware driving guidance and optional auto-drive fast travel
[*]City-map route overlay, DRIVE / WALK shortcuts and bookmarks
[*]Visit history and quick navigation to saved locations or the last parked car
[*]Indoor exit guidance and automatic walking to the nearest exit
[*]22 interface languages
[/list]

[b]How to update[/b]

[list]
[*]Update and enable [b]LIB BA Player Location 0.11.2+[/b]
[*]Update and enable [b]Voogle Route 0.11.11[/b]
[*]Restart the game after both Workshop items have finished updating
[/list]
